# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, threats, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.
Discuss technical decisions in good faith and focus feedback on the work.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns
privately to the maintainer through the contact information on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/jskDr). Reports will be handled
as confidentially as reasonably possible.

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public.
